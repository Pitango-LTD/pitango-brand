# Pitango Market — brand pack

For animations and visual content. **No prices, costs or stock anywhere.**

## About the business
Pitango Market is a fresh market and Asian grocery in southern Israel (Kadesh Barnea and Arad), with a retail store and wholesale delivery by our own vans. We bring fresh vegetables, herbs and chilies from local farms, together with Thai and Asian specialty products, drinks and snacks. The store is warm and simple: a white building with a red door, yellow shopping baskets, wooden benches and tables outside where people meet at sunset. Our customers speak Hebrew, English and Thai.

## Brand style
Fresh, warm, honest and colorful. Never show prices, price tags or numbers.
